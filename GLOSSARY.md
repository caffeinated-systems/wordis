# Wordle

A word-guessing game: the player has a limited number of guesses to find a hidden five-letter word.

## Language

**Answer**:
The five-letter word the player is trying to find. There is one answer per day, shared by every player.
_Avoid_: target, solution, the word

**Guess**:
A five-letter word the player submits as an attempt to find the answer.
_Avoid_: attempt, try

**Dictionary**:
The fixed list of words accepted as guesses. Every answer is in the dictionary.
_Avoid_: word list, valid words
